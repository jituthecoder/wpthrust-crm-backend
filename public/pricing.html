Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is your 90+ Speed Guarantee?
We guarantee your website will achieve a 90+ score on Google PageSpeed Insights (Mobile & Desktop). If we fail to reach the agreed performance target, we provide a 100% money-back refund.
2. Will optimization break my website design, fonts, or layout?
No, absolutely not! We perform all speed optimizations carefully on a staging environment first. We test all interactive elements, checkout forms, and media layouts to ensure 100% functional and visual preservation.
3. How long does the optimization process take?
Most WordPress, Shopify, and custom PHP websites are fully optimized within 24 to 48 hours after login access is provided to our performance engineers.
4. Do I need to buy expensive plugins or pay recurring monthly fees?
No! All our speed optimization packages are a one-time fee. We configure lifetime premium caching and optimization tools so you never have to pay monthly subscriptions for speed plugins.
5. Do you optimize custom PHP, Laravel, and E-Commerce stores like WooCommerce & Shopify?
Yes! We specialize in custom PHP, CodeIgniter, Laravel, Shopify, Magento, and WooCommerce stores. We optimize heavy database queries, liquid/PHP code execution, image compression, and server-level caching.
6. What access details do you need to start optimization?
We only require temporary Administrator access to your website dashboard (e.g., WordPress or Shopify admin) and cPanel / Hosting access if server-level caching or CDN setup is required.
7. How does website speed affect my Google SEO rankings & sales?
Google explicitly uses Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) as an essential search ranking factor. Faster websites rank higher on search engines, reduce bounce rates, and significantly increase ecommerce conversion rates.
8. What is included in your Content Writing & Copywriting services?
Our content services include 100% original, plagiarism-free, SurferSEO-optimized blog posts and high-converting landing page copy written by experienced writers, including competitor research and formatting.
9. What happens if my site speed slows down in the future after updates?
All our packages include 30 days of complimentary post-optimization support and speed monitoring. If a new plugin or update slows down your site, our team will fine-tune it for free.
10. Is my website data and customer information safe during optimization?
Yes, 100%. We adhere to strict data protection standards and never touch or store private customer data. We always recommend creating a backup and updating credentials after completion.
